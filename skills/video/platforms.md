# Platforms and selling in Indonesia

Where the video is posted changes its ending, what may cover it, and what words sell. Read it when the brief's "where it is posted" is known, and for the ending and contact rows. Platform rules and limits change often: the ones below are how the apps work as this was written; when a detail decides the video (a length limit, a policy), say it is worth checking in the app.

## The ending by destination

The guide's ending (one action, the name, the contact, held at least 2 s) stays. What changes is the action and which contact.

| Posted to                    | The action on screen                                                                 | Watch for                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| TikTok, organic              | "Cek keranjang kuning" when the product is linked in TikTok Shop; else "link di bio" | a phone number or "chat WA" sends viewers off the app; TikTok may show such videos to fewer people. Offer the in-app action first, WA second   |
| Instagram Reels              | "DM 'KOPI'" or "Komen 'MAU'" (a keyword), "link di bio", or the WA number            | a keyword reply adds comments, which the app rewards; the user answers each one, or their DM tool does                                         |
| WhatsApp Status              | "Balas status ini buat pesan"                                                        | viewers already have the number; keep it short (Status has a length limit, check it), text bigger and heavier because Status compresses harder |
| Shopee Video, Shopee Live    | "Klik produk di video" or the live time ("Live jam 19.00, ada voucher")              | the product link is in the app; no outside contact                                                                                             |
| YouTube Shorts               | "Subscribe" or "link di deskripsi"                                                   | Shorts have no end screens; the ending is the video's own last beat                                                                            |
| Paid ad (Meta, TikTok)       | the one action the ad button does ("Chat sekarang" for a click-to-WhatsApp ad)       | the platform lays its button over the bottom of the video: keep the in-video action above the bottom safe zone and never contradict the button |
| Marketplace listing, website | the product name and price                                                           | often plays muted and in a small box: the first frame and the price carry it                                                                   |

- Several destinations: one composition per destination only when the user asks; otherwise end with the action for the main one and put the others in the post caption (`publish` skill).
- A keyword CTA ("Komen 'MAU'") only when the user will answer the comments.

## Loops and the end of the video

Reels and TikTok replay a video as soon as it ends. A sale keeps its held ending: the replay shows the hook again, which is fine. A showcase, menu or process clip with no call to action can loop seamlessly instead (`mosio/video-composition/craft/motion.md`, Loops).

## Safe zones beyond the guide

The guide's 9:16 zones cover Reels and TikTok. On TikTok the right-hand buttons take about 140 px: keep text centred. A paid ad's button covers more of the bottom than an organic post's caption. When unsure, keep the message in the central band (y 250-1500) and centred.

## Writing numbers people use

- **Phone:** group it as customers dial it, "0812-3456-7890", or "+62 812-3456-7890" for overseas buyers. Join the groups with non-breaking hyphens (`&#8209;`) so the number never breaks across lines, and hold it at least 2 s so it can be screenshotted.
- **Prices:** "Rp18.000" (no space, a dot for thousands). "18rb" or "18K" are informal: fine for a creator's casual post, never mixed with "Rp" in one video. A discount reads "-40%"; "s/d 50%" or "hingga 50%" only when it is "up to"; "Mulai dari" only when it is true for the cheapest item. The old price smaller and dimmer than the new one.
- **Time and date:** "19.00 WIB" (a dot, with the zone when buyers are outside the user's city), "Sabtu, 1 Nov". Sale names as people say them: "11.11", "Harbolnas".

## Words

- **One register per video,** read from the brand voice and the user's own messages: "Anda" for a clinic, a bank or B2B; "kamu" for most brands; "Kak" for a shop talking to its buyers; "gue/lo" only for a young Jakarta brand that already talks that way; a regional language only when the user asks.
- **Seller words** are fine when true and in the brand's voice: "ready stock", "PO", "COD", "gratis ongkir", "best seller" (only when the user says it is). "Racun", "worth it" and "no debat" are creator words: they sound forced in a clinic's or a bakery's mouth.
- Write in the user's words. Copy from the user's own captions and chats beats any phrase list.

## The selling calendar

Suggest a timely angle when the date is near and the user sells, never as a push: the twin-date sales (9.9, 10.10, 11.11, 12.12) and Harbolnas; payday from about the 25th to the 1st ("promo gajian") and "tanggal tua" before it; Ramadan (sahur, buka puasa, bukber, THR) and Lebaran; back to school; 17 Agustus; the year end. A season brings its own colours (Ramadan green and gold, 17-an red and white): use them as accents inside the brand's look, not instead of it.

## Claims that need proof

On top of `SKILL.md`, "Truth":

- **Halal:** only when the product is certified; the user says so or the brand records it. Never draw a halal logo yourself.
- **BPOM:** food, drinks, cosmetics, supplements and traditional medicine sold in packages need a registration; show or mention a number only as the user gives it.
- **Health and results:** no cure, weight-loss or medical outcome claims ("menyembuhkan", "turun 5 kg"), and no "sebelum/sesudah" of a body unless the user provides real, consented photos and the platform allows them.
- **Religion and identity:** never use religious symbols as decoration, and keep jokes away from ethnicity, religion and race.
- Ads have stricter rules than organic posts: when a claim decides whether an ad is approved, say so and suggest the safer line.
